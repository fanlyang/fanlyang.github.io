Problem

Method

Parameters → Surrogate → Uncertainty

Oct 2025 — Jan 2026

Uncertainty Quantification with AI-Based Surrogate Models

PyTorch · Monte Carlo · Nonlinear FEM

Problem
Estimate how uncertain material parameters affect structural stress responses without evaluating the full nonlinear FEM model for every sample.
Method
A PyTorch neural-network surrogate approximates the parameter-to-response map; Monte Carlo sampling propagates uncertainty.
Your contribution
Constructed the surrogate and investigated stress dependence on Young’s modulus and yield stress.
Evidence
Evaluated prediction accuracy, stress distributions, and failure probabilities defined by critical response thresholds. Numerical metrics are not reported here.
Limitations
Reliability estimates depend on surrogate accuracy, particularly near failure thresholds. Predictions outside the validated parameter range require further validation.

03 / Parallel scientific computing

Parallel scientific computing

Apr 2025 — Jul 2025

MPI-Based Molecular Dynamics Solver

C++ · MPI · Velocity-Verlet

Problem
Simulate large Lennard–Jones particle systems while controlling interaction-evaluation and parallel communication costs.
Method
Velocity-Verlet time integration, cell and Verlet neighbor lists, and MPI domain decomposition.
Your contribution
Developed the C++ solver, implemented efficient short-range interaction searches, and parallelized the computation.
Evidence
Analyzed strong and weak scaling, communication cost, and parallel efficiency. Neighbor lists give approximately O(N) interaction evaluation under a fixed cutoff and bounded local particle density; no numerical speedup is claimed here.
Limitations
Scaling depends on communication overhead and workload balance. The approximately linear complexity assumes bounded neighbor counts, rather than holding for arbitrary particle distributions.

04 / Exploratory heterogeneous computing

Exploratory heterogeneous computing

Dec 2025 — Apr 2026

Quantum–Classical Hybrid Computing Package

C++ · XACC · HHL · VQLS

Problem
Explore quantum representations of small linear-system solutions and their integration into classical computational workflows.
Method
HHL and Variational Quantum Linear Solver algorithms using quantum simulation, with the XACC framework.
Your contribution
Implemented the algorithms in C++ and investigated intermediate representations for quantum–classical workflows.
Evidence
Investigated the distinction between the classical solution vector and its normalized quantum-state representation, including the difficulty of recovering the full vector.
Limitations
This is an exploratory simulation project for small systems. It does not demonstrate quantum speedup or deployment on physical quantum hardware.
