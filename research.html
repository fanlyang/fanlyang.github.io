Jun 2026 — Present

Deep Operator Networks as Learned Smoothers for Multigrid PDE Solvers

Master's Thesis · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Martin Kronbichler

  • Developed a surface finite element solver in deal.II for variable-coefficient Laplace–Beltrami problems on curved manifolds, including geometric multigrid, adaptive mesh refinement, and discretization-error analysis.
  • Developed and evaluated DeepONet-based residual-to-correction operators as learned smoothers within multigrid, benchmarking them against classical Jacobi and Gauss–Seidel/SSOR smoothers using two-grid and V-cycle convergence.
  • Investigated the relationship between neural training objectives and numerical solver performance through energy-error reduction, coarse-grid interaction, and generalization across mesh resolutions.
  • Explored hybrid learned–classical smoothing strategies that combine data-driven operators with conventional multigrid components to improve robustness across different problem settings.
