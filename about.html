I am a Master’s student in Computational Engineering at Ruhr University Bochum, specializing in numerical methods for partial differential equations, finite element methods, multigrid solvers, and high-performance computing.

My current research integrates operator learning with multigrid PDE solvers. My broader interests include numerical analysis, scientific machine learning, and scalable computational methods.

Research interests

  • Numerical PDEs and finite element methods
  • Multigrid solvers and numerical error analysis
  • Operator learning and scientific machine learning
  • Parallel and heterogeneous scientific computing

Current research

Parallel Deep Operator Network for Large-Scale Multigrid PDE Solvers

June 2026–Present
Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Martin Kronbichler

I developed a surface finite element solver for a variable-coefficient Laplace–Beltrami problem using deal.II and investigate learned operators as alternatives to classical multigrid smoothers. My work examines numerical performance and error, including when hybrid strategies are more robust than learned smoothers alone.
