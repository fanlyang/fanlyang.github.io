About me

Fan Yang

I’m Fan Yang, a Computational Engineering Master’s student at Ruhr University Bochum. I study how numerical methods and learned operators can work together to solve complex physical problems.

My work focuses on numerical methods for PDEs, finite element methods, multigrid solvers, scientific machine learning, and high-performance computing.
