Jun 2026 — Present

Parallel Deep Operator Network for Large-Scale Multigrid PDE Solvers

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Martin Kronbichler

  • Developed a surface finite element solver for a variable-coefficient Laplace–Beltrami problem in deal.II, focusing on multigrid methods and error analysis.
  • Investigated DeepONet-based learned operators as alternatives to classical multigrid smoothers.
  • Found that learned smoothers do not universally outperform classical approaches; hybrid strategies provide more robust performance across different problem settings.
